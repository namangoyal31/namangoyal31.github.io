# The homepage animation

## What it represents

A family of analyticity contours in the complex Mandelstam t-plane, drawn as a
living research-paper figure: nested ellipses sharing a pinned right focus and
a pinned right extremity, evoking domains of analyticity in t of the kind used
in Lehmann/Martin-type arguments.

## The mathematics (verified)

Every ellipse has its right focus fixed at the origin, F_R = 0, and its
rightmost extremity fixed at t = mu_0 > 0. With the left focus at t = -c_j
(c_j > 0), the required semi-axes are

    a_j = mu_0 + c_j/2
    b_j = sqrt(mu_0 (mu_0 + c_j))  =  sqrt(a_j^2 - (c_j/2)^2)

because the focal distance is sqrt(a_j^2 - b_j^2) = c_j/2. The center is at
-c_j/2, and the parametrization is

    t_j(phi) = -c_j/2
               + (mu_0 + c_j/2) cos(phi)
               + i sqrt(mu_0 (mu_0 + c_j)) sin(phi).

Two invariants follow immediately for every member of the family:

    t_j(0) = mu_0          (right extremity, pinned)
    foci at 0 and -c_j     (right focus, pinned)

Varying c_j slides the left focus and reshapes the contour while both pins
hold. The code draws four members (three on small screens) with increasing
c_j, so the family reads as related analyticity domains rather than arbitrary
ellipses. Both coordinate axes are drawn through the origin t = 0, with a
small origin marker and a tick at t = mu_0.

## The parabolic limit

The eccentricity of member j is

    e_j = (c_j/2) / a_j = c_j / (c_j + 2 mu_0)  ->  1   as c_j -> infinity.

In the same limit the ellipse opens up and approaches the parabola with
focus t = 0 and vertex t = mu_0, i.e. (with x the real direction from the
origin and y the imaginary direction)

    y^2 = 4 mu_0 (mu_0 - x),

which crosses the imaginary axis at y = +-2 mu_0. The family is chosen so
this limit is visible: c/mu_0 = 0.5, 1.5, 4.5, 15 (eccentricities 0.20,
0.43, 0.69, 0.88), running from a compact ellipse to a contour that is
essentially the parabola within the visible region. The limiting parabola
itself is also drawn as the faintest reference curve, slightly outside the
last member. Nothing is scaled uniformly: every curve is generated by its
own c_j with the two pinned invariants.

## Color

The contour family is drawn in the site accent, a single warm yellow
(`--accent`), with opacity descending from the innermost contour outward
(0.5 to 0.3 on desktop). The axes, mu_0 tick, and the limiting parabola stay
neutral ink. The palette follows the site theme: deep ochre contours on the
light paper variant, warm yellow on the dark default; the canvas re-reads
the tokens whenever the theme attribute changes.

## Motion

The only time dependence is a slow breathing of each c_j:

    c_j(t) = c_j0 * (1 + 0.054 * sin(t * TAU + phase_j)),   T = 3.6 s

which slides the left focus and reshapes the contour while the pins hold
exactly (a_j, b_j, and the center are recomputed from c_j every frame, so
t_j(0) = mu_0 and F_R = 0 are preserved identically). One sinusoid per
ellipse with period T makes the loop repeat without a visible jump.

## Interaction

None. The figure is a background element: it has no pointer interaction and no reset
state. The family breathes on its own loop and opens slightly with scroll; visitors watch
it, they do not operate it.

Governing rule for this site: interactions may change only parameters and
relationships that exist in the underlying mathematics - presentation is
free, invented physics is not.

## The atmosphere system

The animation is one layer of a single visual system that spans the site:

- **Atmosphere** (`.atmosphere`, fixed behind all content): fine static grain
  (a tiled SVG noise plate at 5% opacity, unchanged), a permanent uniform
  complex-plane grid (one consistent weight, lines every 96 px at 3%
  opacity - a single coordinate system, no primary/secondary squares), and
  cursor-proximity illumination: a broad, heavily diffused luminance pool
  (peak ~4.5%) that follows the pointer with a slow lerp, so the plane is
  barely brighter where the cursor is. Touch devices and reduced motion
  keep a static pool. Pressing **G** (outside a text field) crossfades an
  accent-tinted, stronger version of the same uniform grid.
- **Dividers** (`src/components/Divider.astro`): the rule between major
  sections is one straight, continuous line drawn in the site accent, with
  gray pole ticks crossing it. On entering the viewport the rule draws
  itself left to right (SVG pathLength dash transition), then the ticks
  fade in. No detours: hierarchy comes from typography, not the rule's
  shape. Variants pass different pole fractions.
- **Scroll-linked domain**: the hero contour family opens slightly with page
  scroll (c_j gains up to +12%), so exploring the site literally enlarges the
  analyticity domain. Both pinned invariants still hold; the figure also
  fades gently as the hero leaves the viewport.
- **Reveals**: section heads, rows, and figures enter once with a 14px rise,
  staggered by explicit `--reveal-delay` values. Progressive enhancement:
  content is hidden only when the `js` class is present, and all of it is
  disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Technology

Plain Canvas 2D, no libraries, no assets. Source: `src/lib/amp-field.js`,
mounted by a small `<script>` at the bottom of `src/pages/index.astro`. Each
Colors are read from the `--ink` and `--accent` design tokens; the site is dark-only.

## Static preview and reduced motion

- `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` renders one static frame (no loop).
- `?static=1` does the same in a normal browser.
- `?static=1&phase=x` renders the static frame at loop phase x in [0, 1)
  (the breathing offsets correspond to that phase).

## Parameters

All in `src/lib/amp-field.js`:

- `LOOP_MS`: loop length (3.6 s).
- `layout()`: origin position, mu_0 (pixel distance to the pinned right
  extremity), the list of c_j multipliers, per-ellipse phases and alphas, and
  the compact three-ellipse mobile variant below 700 px width.
- Alpha values in `render()`: axes `0.07`, origin marker `0.5` (accent),
  mu_0 tick `0.2`, limiting parabola `0.06`. Ellipse alphas live in
  `layout()` (0.5 down to 0.3 on desktop, innermost most visible).
- Breathing amplitude: the `0.054` factor in `render()`.
- `c` multipliers in `layout()`: 0.5, 1.5, 4.5, 15 (in units of mu_0), which
  set the progression toward the parabolic limit.

## Performance

- One canvas, one rAF loop, no per-frame allocations, DPR capped at 1.5.
- The loop pauses when the hero leaves the viewport (IntersectionObserver) and
  when the tab is hidden (visibilitychange).
- Added weight: ~5 KB of JavaScript, no network requests.
- To disable entirely, remove the `<canvas class="hero-field">` and the
  `<script>` block from `src/pages/index.astro`; nothing else references it.
